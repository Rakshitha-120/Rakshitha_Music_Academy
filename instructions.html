ACADEMY GUIDELINES

Learn Music With Passion & Discipline.

At Rakshitha Music Academy, learning music is more than attending classes. Regular practice, discipline, respect and a positive attitude help every student grow with confidence and enjoy their musical journey.

START YOUR JOURNEY

Simple Steps For Better Learning.

Follow these basic instructions to make every class productive, comfortable and enjoyable.

01
♪

Attend Regularly

Attend your classes regularly and arrive on time. Consistent attendance helps you understand every lesson and maintain steady progress.

02
♫

Practice Daily

Spend some time practicing every day. Regular practice improves your confidence, technique, rhythm and musical understanding.

03
♬

Listen Carefully

Pay attention to your instructor during demonstrations. Listen carefully and ask questions whenever something is not clear.

04
♩

Bring Materials

Bring your required notebooks, music materials and instrument whenever your course requires them.

05
𝄞

Follow Guidance

Follow the practice methods and instructions provided by your music instructor to develop your skills correctly.

06

Enjoy Learning

Music takes time and patience. Enjoy the process, celebrate your improvement and keep developing your musical skills.

ACADEMY RULES

A respectful and organized environment allows students and instructors to focus better on music learning.

✓

Be On Time

Arrive a few minutes before your scheduled class.

✓

Respect Instructors

Listen to instructors and follow classroom guidance.

✓

Respect Fellow Students

Maintain a friendly and supportive learning environment.

✓

Take Care Of Instruments

Handle academy instruments and equipment carefully.

✓

Keep The Classroom Clean

✓

Keep Phones Silent

Do's & Don'ts.

Don't Forget
