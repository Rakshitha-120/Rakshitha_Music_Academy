Our Artists

At Shahin Music Academy, we believe that great music begins with inspiration. We celebrate the artists, musicians and teachers whose dedication, creativity and love for music inspire the next generation of learners.

Veena Srivani

🎵 Veena Artist • Music Educator

A dedicated Veena artist who represents the beauty of Indian classical instrumental music. Her work demonstrates the elegance, discipline and expressive power of the Veena.

Inspiration for Shahin Music Academy:
Her dedication to the Veena inspires our students to appreciate traditional instruments and develop patience, practice and musical expression.

A. R. Rahman

🎹 Composer • Music Producer • Music Director

A globally recognized Indian composer and music producer known for combining Indian musical traditions with contemporary sounds and innovative music production.

Inspiration for Shahin Music Academy:
His creativity encourages our students to explore composition, technology, experimentation and the many possibilities of modern music.

Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia

🪈 Bansuri Maestro • Indian Classical Musician

One of India's celebrated classical flautists, renowned for bringing the expressive beauty of the Bansuri to audiences around the world and for nurturing students through traditional musical learning.

Inspiration for Shahin Music Academy:
His journey reminds our students of the importance of guru-shishya tradition, continuous practice, discipline and devotion to music.

🎤 Singers Who Inspire Us

At Shahin Music Academy, we believe that every great singer begins with passion, practice and a love for music. The journeys of these celebrated singers inspire our students to develop their own voice, confidence and musical expression.

Arijit Singh

🎤 Playback Singer • Vocalist

Arijit Singh is celebrated for his soulful voice, emotional expression and versatility. His musical journey inspires aspiring singers to connect deeply with every song they perform.

Inspiration for Shahin Music Academy:
His dedication to music inspires our students to practice regularly, sing with confidence and express genuine emotion through their voice.

K. S. Chithra

🎤 Playback Singer • Classical Vocalist

K. S. Chithra is admired for her melodious voice, expressive singing and versatility across Indian languages and musical styles.

Inspiration for Shahin Music Academy:
Her musical journey encourages our students to build strong vocal foundations, respect classical music and develop their own unique style.

Shreya Ghoshal

🎤 Playback Singer • Vocalist

Shreya Ghoshal is renowned for her melodious voice, vocal versatility and expressive performances. Her journey is an inspiration to students who dream of developing their singing abilities.

Inspiration for Shahin Music Academy:
Her achievements inspire our students to improve vocal control, musical expression, confidence and their lifelong love for singing.
