ABOUT OUR ACADEMY

Dance Is More Than Movement

Harika Dance Academy is dedicated to teaching dance with passion, discipline and creativity.

Our goal is to provide quality dance education for children, teenagers and adults while helping every student discover their own unique style.

We believe dance develops confidence, concentration, fitness and emotional expression.

Our Mission

To create confident dancers through professional training and a positive learning environment.

Our Vision

To preserve traditional dance while encouraging modern creativity and innovation.

Our Values

Discipline, creativity, respect, confidence and passion.
